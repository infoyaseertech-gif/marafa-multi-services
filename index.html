ABOUT THE COMPANY

A single, capable contractor for a wide range of needs

Marafa Multi-Services Limited (RC 9797637) is a private company limited by shares, duly incorporated under the Companies and Allied Matters Act 2020, with its registered office in Unguwar Rimi, Kaduna State. We were formed on a simple premise: clients across Northern Nigeria need a dependable partner who can move between trade, construction, logistics and advisory work without losing accountability along the way.

From general merchandise and import/export to construction, transportation and consultancy, our registered scope of business was deliberately built wide.
