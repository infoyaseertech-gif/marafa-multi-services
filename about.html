OUR STORY

Formed for versatility, run with accountability

Marafa Multi-Services Limited (RC 9797637) is a private company limited by shares, duly incorporated under the Companies and Allied Matters Act 2020, with its registered office in Unguwar Rimi, Kaduna State.

The Company was conceived on the premise that many clients — particularly in Northern Nigeria — need a single, capable, trustworthy contractor able to deliver across multiple service lines, rather than engaging several fragmented vendors. We were therefore structured with a broad scope of registered business activities, giving us the legal and operational flexibility to serve diverse client needs under one corporate umbrella.

Anchored on integrity, reliability and a firm commitment to quality service delivery, we are positioned to grow into a trusted name for general merchandise, supplies, contracting and support services within Kaduna State and, progressively, across Nigeria.
